## What changed in brand/BRAND.md because of these

- **Captions:** short phrases of 2 to 4 words on a solid Umber or Ink plate, with an italic serif emphasis word. This replaces the shadow-only default.
- **Keyword titles:** one huge Newsreader word over the frame on key beats.
- **Title stack:** a Hanken Grotesk Bold uppercase line over a Newsreader Italic line ending in a full stop.
- **Split-screen layout** for screen recordings and B-roll.
- **Grade:** warm, natural, soft contrast (reel 3), not the dark cool look of reel 1.

## Can't do yet

Text behind the speaker needs a subject matte (segmentation). That isn't set up; see Known gaps in BRAND.md.

# Style Lab brief references (2026-10-17)

These replace the earlier mix as the brief's references.

1. **Tom Noske, instagram.com/reel/DeSo34eBhvZ.** Loved:
   - Cut scenes with a plain background and text over the top. Cards are deep green-black `#121D1D` with sand-cream text `#E0D0C0`, or sand `#E4D1BF` with near-black `#101010` text, in bold sans with tight tracking.
   - His caption style: 1 to 2 bold lowercase words, centred, light text.
   - The Helvetica look.
   - Also present: big stacked bold words ("seven million", "PROFITABLE NICHE"), a Venn diagram, big numbers ("0.3", "$10,000") and quote marks ("BUILD").
2. **Gannon Meyer, instagram.com/reel/DeK5cgNskkb.** Loved:
   - The animation style on a plain white background: a line chart drawing up to "$82,042", floating avatar bubbles, a stats card with a donut ("49%"), clean UI cards.
   - The 50/50 split: white top with animation, speaker below.
   - His caption style: tiny bold white words on the seam or over the picture.
3. **Jason Cooperson, instagram.com/reel/DdovWa7D35X.** Loved: the split-screen elements and the animations (UI cards and icons coming in). Disliked: the dark grid ("hex") background on plain-background moments, and his spaced mono captions.
4. **RPN, instagram.com/reel/DecWaVFJC4v.** What it does:
   - The top half is almost always moving footage: product shots, screen recordings, B-roll cut every 1 to 2 s.
   - The speaker sits in the bottom half against a dark studio with purple and blue practicals.
   - A small spaced uppercase caption sits on the seam ("THIS MODEL.").
   - Occasional full-frame B-roll and a clean product end card.
   - What the user loves: the split-screen elements and the animations (same as Cooperson).
