# cooperson-split

Learned from jasoncooperson (instagram.com/reel/DdovWa7D35X).

**Feel:** product demo. Clean, techy and confident.

- **Layout:** split screen while the speaker is talking. An Ink `#141313` panel fills the top 940 px with a faint Stone grid (60 px, 7% opacity). The speaker is reframed into the bottom half: the clip is moved down 740 px so the face sits just under the seam. B-roll plays full frame with no panel.
- **Captions:** DM Mono Medium, UPPERCASE, 42 px, +12% tracking, white, 2 to 3 words, with a soft Ink edge. No plate and no karaoke. During split they sit just inside the panel (y 870), because white on a pale backdrop disappears. Over full-frame B-roll they sit at y 960.
- **Panel graphics:** one per beat, changing on the beat.
  - Big Hanken ExtraBold title with a mono sub-line.
  - App-style checklist cards: dark `#1C1A19` cards with a Stone icon tile, a label, a mono sub-line and a green check.
  - A Vale Studio identity block (Newsreader wordmark with a NAME · ROLE · PLACE mono line).
- **Grade:** Vale Warm Natural. The reference is dark and cool, but we stay warm.
- **No glows or gradients**, even though the reference uses some.
