# herk-bold

Learned from nateherkai (instagram.com/reel/DdT_OwyFeoj).

**Feel:** fast, loud and clear. Built for hooks and tips.

- **Captions:** Hanken Grotesk ExtraBold, UPPERCASE, 78 px, 1 to 3 words per beat, white on an Ink `#141313` block (6 px corners). Use the no-plate version with a soft Ink shadow over busy B-roll. Plate top at y 1470 on tight framing.
- **Punch-ins:** cut to 115% framing on the hook payoff and the call to action. Cut, don't animate the zoom. At most one every 6 s.
- **Numbered beats:** "#1", "#2" in Newsreader italic, cream `#F4F0EC`, about 150 px, for listicles.
- **Split screen** for screen recordings: recording on top and the speaker below, like cooperson-split, with bold captions on the seam.
- **Pace:** the fastest of the three. Trim every pause over 0.2 s (the cut list decides this).
- **Grade:** Vale Warm Natural.
