# justyn-editorial

Learned from justyn.ai (instagram.com/reel/DeUUFJQu8cw). This is the Vale default.

**Feel:** editorial and calm. Like a magazine spread that talks.

- **Captions:** Newsreader Regular 54 px, sentence case, 3 to 5 words, on a tight Umber `#5A504A` plate (16/10 px padding, 3 px corners). Karaoke: spoken words are cream `#F4F0EC` and upcoming words are dim `#C2B5A6`. Plate top at y 1470.
- **Keyword:** one huge Newsreader word (about 250 px) in the top band on the hook, with a DM Mono date line above it ("16 · 10 · OCTOBER 2026 · SUNSHINE COAST"). Ink on light backgrounds, cream on dark ones.
- **Title stack:** HANKEN EXTRABOLD CAPS (92 px, cream) over a *Newsreader italic line.* (96 px, `#E6D5BC`), each on its own Umber plate, left at x 90. Use it for the closing line or a chapter break.
- **Note card:** a cream rounded card with a mono label, one sans line and a round Umber arrow button. Use it for the offer or a key fact.
- **Lower third:** Newsreader name and mono role on an Umber plate with a light stone rule. Put it in the top band when captions are running.
- **Pill:** a white pill with one bold Umber span, for a number ("**1 day** of filming").
- **Layout:** full frame. Titles in the top band (y 70 to 400), captions in the bottom band (y 1470). Never stack them in the same band.
- **Pace:** hard cuts, at most one title beat every 6 to 8 s.
- **Palette alternative:** "forest" (`#183429` plate, `#C4AC5C` accent) is the reference's own colours. Use it only if asked.
